A COMPARISON THAT LOOKS OBVIOUS AND IS WRONG — recorded so it is not re-derived

  Comparing sheet B's 'Position/s' against the store's {present_position, alternate_position}
  yields 381 differences of 804.  THAT NUMBER IS MEANINGLESS.  The two are different concepts:
  'Position/s' is an unordered ELIGIBILITY LIST (a player may carry three or four codes), while
  the store pair is a present assignment plus AT MOST ONE alternate.  Sheet B's first-listed code
  equals the store's present_position for only 531 of 804, so the list is not even ordered
  present-first.  This is the 556 defect in a new costume: same shape, different pair of columns.

  The owner's sheets carry NO present_position column at all, so there is nothing in them to
  compare against store.present_position.  Stating that gap rather than inventing a comparison.
