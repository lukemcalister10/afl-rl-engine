## P14 — THE CEREMONY IS PRICED TO THE ACT (proportional gating)

> P14. **THE CEREMONY IS PRICED TO THE ACT.** Every act declares up front — in its prereg,
> or in its opening register entry when no prereg is owed — (a) its blast radius: which
> value-bearing artifacts can move; and (b) its wall-clock budget. The ceremony then scales
> to the declared radius in BOTH directions: a process or tooling act does not run a
> value-act's battery, and a value-moving act does not skip one — law 11 already draws that
> line for review, this law draws it for everything else. **EXCEEDING THE DECLARED BUDGET IS
> A RECORDED FAILURE**: the closing register entry names the overrun and its cause the same
> way it names a red gate. Slow used to be free because it was unrecorded, while wrong was
> recorded and feared; that asymmetry is how ceremony grows without limit.
> *Incident: the ORDER 45 landing, 2026-08-25 — ten attempts around 53 minutes of actual
> machine work, the owner awake through the night for it ("It should not take 8 attempts to
> land something"), under his standing directive: "No convenient interpretation of my words
> to be lazy. We don't unnecessarily run machine time or actual time when it could be made
> efficient. Parallelise. Load things once."*

## P15 — A GUARD IS PROVEN ABLE TO FIRE BEFORE IT IS TRUSTED

> P15. **A GUARD IS PROVEN ABLE TO FIRE BEFORE IT IS TRUSTED.** No check, gate, mirror,
> monitor, or validator is cited as coverage until it has been shown to RED on a
> deliberately broken input — its corrupt leg — and that firing is filed in the evidence of
> the act that introduces or repairs it. A guard whose only recorded history is green is
> UNPROVEN, whatever it claims to measure. P5 says named-is-not-run; this law says
> run-is-not-read. Falsifier: replay the filed corrupt leg against the current instrument;
> it fires today, or it reds the run that cites it.
> *Incident: the ORDER 45 window carried five confident instruments measuring the wrong
> thing — among them acceptance m1a's mirror_parity, which returned its verdict while unable
> to PARSE either side of the mirror it certifies (attempt 7), the movers test asserting a
> hand-typed boundary count (attempt 8), and the spec validator accepting a string where a
> dict of env vars belongs (attempt 1). The one instrument in that window that had a filed
> corrupt-leg demonstration — falsifier 3 — was the one whose verdict needed no
> re-derivation.*

## THE HANDOVER DIET (his rule #3, TENTATIVE — sample for the word)

Ruled #1 (2026-08-25): a fresh seat is bound by the rulebook and the owner's words only.
Ruled #2: dead process gets a tombstone, not a survival. Tentative #3 is the diet itself —
what a compaction handover may carry. Proposed as a process note (not a numbered law until
it has survived a few compactions):

**A handover carries at most:** (1) the board-of-record line — every pinned identity, one
line; (2) open owner words — asked and unanswered, or ruled and not yet executed, verbatim;
(3) the act in flight — its register anchor and the single next step; (4) tombstones — one
line each for anything a prior seat carried that is now dead, so it cannot re-bind;
(5) pointers, never prose — the register version and evidence paths, not restatements of
their contents. **Everything else the fresh seat re-derives from the tree.** A handover that
narrates process, restates rules in its own words, or carries seat-lore ("we usually…") is
over the diet — the lore is exactly what his compaction diagnosis showed re-binding fresh
seats to purged process.

**Sample handover under the diet (the whole thing):**
> BOARD: 3167cba6 @ 50903d4 · engine d84031cf · store fb640ca0 · config 29fdfd1e · seal
> de37f057 · round 24 HELD.
> OPEN WORDS: packet words owed — depth-3 cap, ruck relief, pedigree mechanism (after
> decomposition), smoothing confirmation. Amendments P14/P15 + diet + budget: word pending.
> IN FLIGHT: the speed act (register v863 opener) — next step: land the preflight command.
> TOMBSTONES: the derived twin (v3), FADE30B_D (stale one generation), the 8-pair pedigree
> class (v862 — dissolved; capital-compression is the live framing).
> POINTERS: register newest v86x · RULEBOOK v3 · evidence docs/evidence/<act>/.
