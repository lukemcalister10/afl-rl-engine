The owner cut the design down and was right to.** Owner word: *"We're literally just updating player averages and game counts. Before round 14 we didn't add games one by one, we just priced based off averages and total season game counts. It really shouldn't be that hard."*

THE LANE WAS THE DEFECT. FW1 was built as a round-advance that HOLDS the calendar — `RL_CALENDAR_ROUNDS`, a feed/calendar split, a finals ceiling, finals-aware postconditions in eleven consumers, `previous_point`, movers-page labels, browser-side coherence. Three flights, thirteen aborts. Then the owner pointed at the store, and the store agreed with him: Bontempelli's 2016 row reads 26 games (22 home-and-away + 4 finals), his 2021 reads 26. **A finals game has never been a calendar event here. It is two numbers on a season row.**

WHAT MODELLING IT AS A ROUND COST. "A round" drags in the dedup ledger, finalization state, round history, movers keyed by round — and a recomputation of `season_state`. That last one produced the defect: `calendar_progress` fell 1.00 -> **0.83**, because the hold put the numerator at 24 and left the denominator on the FEED bound of 29. It is a live pricing dial (`rl_model.SEASON_PROG`, `conditional_prior._SFE`, `_merged_recover.M3_FE`; 1.0 turns the lever OFF), so it would have re-opened every completed season in the competition — the exact punishment of non-finalists the owner ruled against, and the exact number three separate comments in this repository warn about, one of them eight lines above the line that caused it. **Found by reading a running transaction's journal, not by a check.** Fixed and asserted (a finals week may not move `calendar_progress`, `as_of_round` or `season_total_rounds` at all), with the disaster value reproduced on purpose in a test so a swap back is a red in milliseconds.

THE LANE NOW. `land edit`, `act_kind: store-edit`: `scoring[2026].games` +1 and a re-averaged `scoring[2026].avg`, 184 edits over 92 players, and nothing else. The career `games` field is untouched because `_merge_into_store` does not touch it either. `season_state` is not recomputed — it is HELD — so non-finalists are safe BY CONSTRUCTION rather than by a hold applied correctly in eleven places. Re-applying the same week halts on `old` no longer matching, which is the protection the dedup ledger was providing.

TWO MEASUREMENT DEFECTS, BOTH CAUGHT BY DECLARING THINGS. (1) The first preview rounded averages to ONE decimal; the ingestor's own `ROUND_DECIMALS` is 2 and the store carries two. The new plan CALLS `_mean` rather than restating the formula. (2) The in-memory prediction claimed **86 movers and a ripple of exactly zero**; the builder found **234**, because pricing in memory cannot reproduce the LOAD-TIME CALIBRATION REFIT — move 92 players' averages and the population statistics the model prices against move too. The declaration caught it and the abort restored every carrier byte-exact. Claiming no ripple was the interesting thing to be wrong about, and nothing but `expected_movers` would have found it.

THE RIPPLE, MEASURED, because the ruling is about non-finalists: median **1 point (0.082% of value)**, mean 1.9, 90th 4, max 8 (Sheezel, -0.07%); 5 of 146 moved by more than 5 points; direction mixed, some gain. Re-calibration noise, not a penalty — and a different animal from the `calendar_progress` stretch, which was systematic.

THE OWNER'S CONSTRAINT, CHECKED NOT ASSUMED: *"as long as it doesn't impact the movers list still being accurate as of each round since round 14."* Two things could hurt it, and both were verified against the record. The per-round reports R15-R24 are frozen history a store edit does not touch (proven on the previous store edit: all ten byte-identical). The RETROSPECTIVE series IS dropped by any act that rebuilds the bundle — `points` is built from the value-history columns and a retro point is not one — which happened at f1026eb and was repaired at b53cde1 by a person noticing. The emitter replays BANKED per-round values and does not re-price, so restoring it is byte-exact; proven idempotent on the live tree before the flight. It is now a TEST against the shipped bundle, so the loss can never be silent again.
