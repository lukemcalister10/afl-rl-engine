### ITEM C — the cap release under the evidence weight (ruling 3.8, D3). THE ROAD TO 1.04.
THE DESIGN (returns for the owner's eyes — this text IS the deliverable, refined at delivery):

**The weight.** w(player, N) = G × Q, gated by z. Three parts, each already an engine object:
- **G, the games term** (more games is more evidence): G = g/(g + G0), saturating. G0 ≈ 8
  teaching-chosen. 2 games → ~0.20 · 6 games → ~0.43 · 14 games → ~0.64 · 20+ → ~0.7+. Never
  reaches 1 in year 1 — a season cannot fully prove a career.
- **Q, the quality term** (higher average is greater evidence — measured against WHO HE IS):
  Q = clip(sa / par(pos, draft-age), 0, Q_max). par = the existing pedigree par surface
  (position × age), NOT raw pick — so an age-18 KPF at pick 49 is judged against age-18-KPF
  par, which is exactly the Ludowyke case: 2 games at par-for-his-kind reads as a GOOD signal,
  small-but-positive w. Q_max ≈ 2 stops one hot game buying the whole release.
- **The z gate** (context: demonstrated growth vs his own entry): z = log(e/entry_anchor), the
  probe's sharpest separator (3.6× harder than sa). The gate scales w down toward 0 for players
  whose overall record sits below entry expectation — this is what protects the five top-pick
  fallers (their falls halved under the z-gated surface in the probe) and kills the
  1-game-at-7 case (G≈0.1, Q≈0.1, z low → w ≈ 0).
- **The spirit test, answered in the arithmetic**: 1 game at 120 (G 0.11 × Q ~2 capped = w
  ~0.22·gate) vs 6 games at 70 (G 0.43 × Q ~1.2 = w ~0.52·gate) — the six games win, "maybe
  not" resolved the way the owner leaned.

**The release.** ceiling(player) = entry_anchor × (1 + w × (H − 1)), where H is the headroom
bound. w = 0 → the old cap EXACTLY (the guard holds for no-evidence rows — the sit-out
population is untouched by construction, lam=0 rows have g=0 → G=0). H is set by the no-arb
frame: the cohort landing must enter [1.04, 1.13] without creating a year-1→peak arbitrage
(the 1.0945 pivot); H teaches as the ONE new dial, sized at the sitting from the final
side-by-side ladder. Book conservation: the release is funded within the cohort book by the
ITEM H cuts and the conservation re-teach — the sums shown in the side-by-side.

**Consumers**: the year-1+ ND ceiling (primary); the RUCK prior cap (ITEM E — same w, so a
young no-evidence ruck stays capped and a producing ruck earns headroom). NOT the sit charge
(ITEM D is a separate, conserved tilt).

**Double-counting guard**: w consumes sa only THROUGH Q-vs-par; the stage-6 surface (if F1
ships) consumes sa as a shape gate on a different object (the taught residual). The build
asserts the two never multiply on the same leg.
