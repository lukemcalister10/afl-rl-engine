2 · THE TILT. ONE EQUATION, AND IT IS THE MEASUREMENT.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   With T linear in s inside the exponent, the delivered slope is exactly constant:

       d ln(retained pedigree) / ds  =  LAMBDA * A(g) * THETA_R

   Setting LAMBDA * THETA_R = BETA_sat makes the pedigree leg respond to the pedigree-conditional
   surplus at exactly the rate the outcome data says it should, at every level of s, scaled by
   A(g), which reproduces the measured BETA_P(g) = BETA_sat * A(g).

       LAMBDA * THETA_R = BETA_sat = 0.11465     90% CI [0.10416, 0.12718]

   That is ONE equation. LAMBDA is solved by the anchoring identity in Step 4. THETA_R follows as
   BETA_sat / LAMBDA. There is no free parameter left and nothing to tune.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3 · s0 AND THE CAP, FROM THE POPULATION'S OWN SPREAD
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   s0 = the GAMES-WEIGHTED mean of the NEW surplus over the young cohort = -2.4527 points per game.
        (ORDER N, on the age-only surplus: +2.3163. The centre moves because the bar moved.)
   unweighted mean -7.7351 · median -6.8475 · p5 -33.06 · p95 +14.38
   T(s0) = 1 by construction: a row producing exactly at the cohort centre pays the base charge.

   THE CAP. TMAX is the value T takes at the cohort's own 5th percentile of the new surplus,
   -33.06 points per game, so the worst-producing 5% all pay the same top rate rather than an
   unbounded one. The bound is the data's own spread. TMAX is computed in Step 4, where THETA_R
   becomes known.

   THE LOWER CLIP AT ZERO. A young player far enough above his PEDIGREE-CONDITIONAL bar pays no
   charge on his pedigree leg at all. The surplus at which that happens is s0 + 1/THETA_R and it
   is reported in Step 4.

4 · THE PREREGISTERED STRUCTURAL PROPERTIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   P-S1  A(0) = 0 exactly, so pi(0) = D and no day-0 print can move         PASS
   P-S2  A is non-decreasing in g over [0, 400]                             PASS
   P-S3  T is non-increasing in s                                           PASS
   P-S4  the factor exp(-LAMBDA*A*T) is in (0,1] for every non-negative argument PASS
   P-S3 and P-S4 are structural: T is a clipped decreasing line, and exp(-x) is in (0,1] for x>=0.
   P-S5 (no row prices above its own uncharged price) is asserted row by row in Step 4.
