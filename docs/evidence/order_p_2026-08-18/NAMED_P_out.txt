   pays more. That is the owner's sentence, on two real rows.

4 · THE DECLARED AGE SENSITIVITY. The premium is NOT age-flat, and this prices what that costs.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Step 2 measured the premium spread PG(p90)-PG(p10) at every age: 19 +8.83 · 20 +14.27 ·
   21 +16.44 · 22 +17.29 · 23 +19.60. The primary bar POOLS ages, as preregistered, because the
   ages 18-19 TALL cells hold 8 and 169 rows. The pooled spread is +16.7 SMALL / +9.8 TALL.
   So the pooled bar is MORE demanding of a 19-year-old high pick than his own age slice says, and
   LESS demanding of a 23-year-old one. This variant carries the age axis and re-solves LAMBDA.
