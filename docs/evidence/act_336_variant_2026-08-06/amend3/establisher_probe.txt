READ IT CAREFULLY — the test's MEANING changed at amendment 2, and BOTH its columns move for reasons that
are not "a charge crept back in". Two separate things are inside the 0.943, and they must not be pooled:

  (i) THE PROBABILITY CHARGE AT THE ANCHOR — the thing this test was built to catch. From amendment 2
      onward an ESTABLISHED player is charged NOTHING there: his anchor factor is D + r x (1-D) with
      r = 1, which is EXACTLY 1.000000, for every establisher on the board, no cell exceptions, no
      smoothing. On this leg the establisher probe is not 1.023 or 0.943 — it is a hard 1.000.

  (ii) THE BASIS MOVE — which is the whole point of the variant and is NOT a charge. `applied` divides
      by the SHIPPED par, and the shipped par is the survivors-at-that-tenure mean. The variant's
      conditional level is de-survivored on the tenure axis (an establisher who faded to three games
      still teaches at his realized level instead of vanishing), so it sits BELOW the shipped number by
      construction: applied runs 0.835-0.975 here, mean 0.919. Divide that by P and you get 0.943 — a
      ratio of two different bases, not a double charge.

  So the honest reading of this table is: the 0.943 is (ii), and (i) is exactly 1.000. The amendment-1
  arithmetic can no longer isolate (i) on its own, because its denominator moved. That is a limitation of
  the OLD test under the NEW basis, stated rather than papered over.
