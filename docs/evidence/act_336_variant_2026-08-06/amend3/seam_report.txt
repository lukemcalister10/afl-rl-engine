== 3. THE GAMES SWEEP — is there a BAR at 6 at all? ==
   A seam is a STEP: the variant/baseline ratio would sit at one level below 6 games and jump at 6.
   Swept G = 1..10 on the same 8 probes, here are the per-step ratios r(g+1)/r(g), pooled:

      1 -> 2    mean  0.9087   worst  0.8386
      2 -> 3    mean  1.0022   worst  1.0593
      3 -> 4    mean  0.9840   worst  0.9572
      4 -> 5    mean  0.9919   worst  1.0592
      5 -> 6    mean  1.0110   worst  1.0933   <== THE ESTABLISHMENT BAR
      6 -> 7    mean  1.0205   worst  1.1268
      7 -> 8    mean  1.0071   worst  1.0205
      8 -> 9    mean  1.0132   worst  1.0361
      9 -> 10   mean  1.0033   worst  0.9389

   THE 5->6 STEP IS INDISTINGUISHABLE FROM ITS NEIGHBOURS. Its mean (1.0110) is smaller than the
   6->7 step (1.0205) and far smaller than the 1->2 step (0.9087); its worst case (1.0933) is
   smaller than 6->7's (1.1268). There is no bar at six. What the DiD picks up is the BASIS move —
   the bust-inclusive tables and the de-survivored conditional level shift the variant/baseline ratio
   at EVERY games count, with curvature, and the DiD cannot net out curvature it sits on.
