#336 AMENDMENT 3 — SINGLE-CHARGE RECONCILIATION on the UNRESOLVED subset.  Required proof.

CAPTION, mandated on every sheet of this act:
  reference layer bust-inclusive; year-zero surface held at the shipped survivors-basis fit;
  the joint re-derivation is #334 stage B, after the ruling.

THE QUESTION (never asked on this population before). Addendum 1 proved establishment risk is charged
ONCE at the anchor for players who go on to ESTABLISH (probe mean excess 1.023). It never asked what
the forward band was already charging the players who have NOT established — and amendment 2 stacked
the full career-level P on top of whatever that was.

== 2. d_band — WHAT THE FORWARD BAND ALREADY CHARGES.  Measured, anchor UNDISCOUNTED on both sides ==
   d_band = ev(unresolved p) / ev(CE(p)),  CE(p) = the same player with establishment made certain.
   Value-weighted (sums of prices): the question is about VALUE, and a mean of ratios is dominated by
   the 60% of this subset whose price the anchor cannot move at all. Both statistics given.

   comparator construction                            sum ev  sum ev_CE    d_band    P (vw)  P/d_band
   MINIMAL  best season -> 6g  (OPERATIVE, conservative)      31752      44866    0.7077    0.7075    0.9996
   every season -> >=6g                                31752      45313    0.7007    0.7079    1.0102
   every season -> >=18g  (full exposure)              31752      50883    0.6240    0.6946    1.1132
   (per-player MEAN of the operative ratio, for completeness: 0.9815, median 1.0000, sd 0.2785)

   The OPERATIVE reading is the CONSERVATIVE one — it moves the least evidence, so it yields the
   smallest comparator and the LARGEST d_band, i.e. it is the reading that most resists the finding
   below. All three readings point the same way.

====================================================================================================================
== 3. THE RECONCILIATION, and THE FINDING ==
   d_band (what the band already takes)            = 0.707707
   target  (the true class risk, career-level P)   = 0.707455
   D = min(1, target / d_band)                     = 0.999644      <- the anchor-side discount

   THE FINDING, and it is the branch the directive named as a possible outcome:
   THE FORWARD BAND ALREADY CHARGES ESTABLISHMENT FAILURE IN FULL, AND THEN SOME.
   0.7077 of charge against a class risk of 0.7075 — the two agree to 0.04%. The anchor owes
   essentially NOTHING further and the reconciliation lands on its floor: D = 0.9996, never negative,
   never lifting the anchor above the conditional level to make room for a band that over-charges.

   CONSEQUENCE, stated plainly: amendment 2's anchor-side P on unresolved players was a SECOND
   charge on top of a band already pricing the whole risk — the same double charge Addendum 1
   removed for establishers, one population over. Amendment 3 removes it for the other population.

== 6. THE PASS-THROUGH — measured, and NOT USED.  Recorded because it is WHY per-cell fails ==
   Scaling the anchor by D does not scale the price by D. With the ramp held at r=0 to isolate it:
     D=0.8 : sum ev 31752 -> 27961 (ratio 0.8806)  elasticity 0.570   anchor-INERT players 194 of 329 (59%)
     D=0.5 : sum ev 31752 -> 26960 (ratio 0.8491)  elasticity 0.236   anchor-INERT players 197 of 329 (60%)
   Strongly saturating, not a power law, and 6 in 10 of this subset do not move AT ALL — they are
   priced off the fixed pick curve, which carries its own bust rate already. No exponent is fitted or
   applied: at D = 0.9996 there is no discount left to pass through.
