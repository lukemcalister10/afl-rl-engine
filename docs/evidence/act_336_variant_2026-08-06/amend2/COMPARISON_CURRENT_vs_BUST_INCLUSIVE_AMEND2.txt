  AMENDMENT 1 (your Robey catch). The first cut discounted every anchor by P(established BY TENURE),
  which at year one is small for everybody because most first-year listees have not yet played six
  games. That is a fact about the calendar, not the player. Fixed: the probability is now CAREER-LEVEL
  P(EVER establishes), tenure entering only as window membership. STILL IN FORCE.

  AMENDMENT 2 (your Trembath/Taylor catch). YOU WERE RIGHT AGAIN, and the definition is the proof.
  Establishment means one season of >=6 games. On that definition ALL EIGHT of the players you named
  have ALREADY ESTABLISHED — Trembath 20g@65.5, Taylor 9/17/18g, Moyle 8g and 13g, Lai 15g, Robey 14g,
  Dean 16g, Madden 6g, Retschko 15g. Amendment 1 was still charging every one of them the chance that
  they never establish. That risk has RESOLVED, in their favour. Fixed:

      ESTABLISHED player  ->  anchored to E[level | ever establishes].   NO probability discount.
      UNRESOLVED player   ->  anchored to P(ever establishes) x E[level | ever establishes].

  Busts stay in every establishment-rate denominator — the rate did not change, only WHO it is applied
  to. And nothing reverts to survivor averaging: the conditional level itself was also de-survivored,
  so an establisher who faded to three games in a season now teaches at his real number instead of
  disappearing from the table.

  THE SITES REPAIRED — every place a real player's own value regresses toward a class reference:
    1 the par-centred level feature (lvl_par)      6 cohort_peak (baseline + own track delta)

  LEFT ALONE ON PURPOSE: everything that prices a PICK or a synthetic. A pick has no resolved state
