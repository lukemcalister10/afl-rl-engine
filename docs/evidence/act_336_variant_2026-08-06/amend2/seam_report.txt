  DESIGN NOTE, per the directive — NOT smoothed here.
    L-SMOOTH forbids a cliff in the shipped engine. Resolved-state conditioning PUTS ONE THERE by
    construction: the anchor multiplies by exactly 1/P the instant a player plays his sixth game
    in a season, and P is as low as 0.41 in the thinnest late-pick cell (KPF 49-99), so the anchor
    can jump 2.43x on one game. In BOARD POINTS the realised jump is smaller (the anchor is only
    one leg of the price and the evidence weights damp it) but still reaches 1.582x on the probes
    above — a +58% price move for one game of football.
    THIS IS A REAL CONFLICT BETWEEN TWO RULES, not an implementation slip:
      * the owner's law here: prices move on news, and establishing IS the news;
      * L-SMOOTH: no cliffs, and the engine already went out of its way to kill the game-6 cliff
        (conditional_prior._lvl_wt: 'Smooth (no >=6g threshold -> kills the game-6 value cliff)').
    A continuous form exists (blend the two anchors on a smooth resolution weight rather than a
    hard >=6g switch) but it is NOT in this variant: the directive rules the binary conditioning,
    and inventing a taper the owner has not ruled on would put an undeclared dial on the board.
    Reported with its magnitude, for the ruling.
