THE POOLING, STATED. Thin strata are neither dropped nor silently trusted. Every rate is shrunk
toward the ALL-POSITION marginal for its band (and, on the par lever, its band x tenure) by

      P = (n_established + 10 * P_marginal) / (n + 10)

i.e. the n/(n+k) design par_build already uses for its ramp shrinkage, with k = 10 pseudo-
observations. At n=246 this moves the rate by under a point; at n=3 it is most of the answer, which
is the intent. Every cell's n travels with the number wherever it is printed.

ONE SUB-DECISION, DISCLOSED. 148 of the 1,287 established players have a >=6-game season but never a
>=10-game one, so the engine's `pkbest` is undefined for them. They ARE established; scoring them 0
would re-import the very defect one bar higher. They contribute their REALIZED level — the same
top-2 mean taken at the >=6-game bar. This is why the variant's POOL row reads 94.0 -> 34.0 where
the sweep's naive pkbest-or-zero construction read 93.2 -> 29.8 (both reproduced on this store).

ONE READING OF THE DIRECTIVE, DISCLOSED. The directive names
`docs/evidence/noarb_338_2026-08-06/per_entrant_338_confirmation.json` as the source of
P(establishes). That file is an EVIDENCE artifact, not on the engine's load path, and the variant's
scope fence is two engine files — so the variant DERIVES P LIVE AT IMPORT from the store, mirroring
the #338 helpers, and the JSON-derived table was computed separately as the audit cross-check.
The two agree closely but NOT exactly: 14 of the 48 (position x band) strata differ, max |dP| =
0.0435. The cause is population, not method — the per_entrant lineage carries 1,969 in_hist records
against the engine's 1,974 (it drops the two force-majeure rows and the _pvc_exclude rows and
de-duplicates), and it bands on the Q-B slid pick where the engine bands on MA.effpk. The LIVE
derivation is the one the variant consumes, because BPK / POOL band on MA.effpk. Both tables are in
the evidence dir (`pest_strata.txt` carries the cross-check row by row).

ONE AXIS MIXTURE, DISCLOSED. On the par lever the LEVEL is keyed by the season position (the E3
ruling) but the PROBABILITY by the settled career position gfut(p). It has to be: a player who never
took the park at tenure T has no season-position for that year.

7. THE HELD BASIS — WHAT IT DOES AND DOES NOT PROTECT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The frozen year-zero surface was HELD, deliberately: v0surf signature af556bdca53d, frozen=True, on
BOTH sides. No RL_V0SURF_REFIT. The surface's signature is blind to the par and BPK tables, so a
refit here would have silently conflated #336 with #334 stage B.

What this protects: national-draft entry prices. Inside the measured population of 1,197 ND picks
1-64, exactly ONE record's year-zero value moved (daniel-butler, the Q-B boundary crosser).

WHAT IT DOES NOT PROTECT, AND THIS MUST BE READ: 1,072 of the 1,201 POOL records DO move at year
zero (max -692 points). The pool entry level is derived from the position layers, which read the
repaired reference tables — it is not on the frozen national curve. So the variant's board carries a
mixed basis by construction: national entry prices held, pool entry prices moved. That is stated,
not hidden, and it is one of the things stage B has to resolve.

A SECOND DISCLOSURE ON THE MEASUREMENT HARNESS. The directive expected the no-arb harness identity
pins to halt on the moved basis. THEY DID NOT. All three were re-measured on the variant matrix and
all three came back unchanged (store 37ced3ce, v0surf af556bdca53d, n=1197), while the matrices
themselves differ (5fb617d0 vs f4b3986a). No assert was patched; each was proven able to fire.
The finding stands on its own: the harness's identity pins CANNOT DISCRIMINATE a reference-layer
basis move — the same blindness _v0surf_sig has, one layer out. Only rl_model / fv separate the two
runs, and they are recorded in MD5S.txt.
