----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE AMENDMENT, STATED FIRST (what changed since the last sheet, and why)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  YOUR CATCH WAS RIGHT, and this run is the answer to it. The first cut applied the probability
  ESTABLISHED-BY-TENURE at the level anchor. At year one that number is small for everybody
  by construction — most first-year listees have not yet played six games — so it charged a
  player for not having done yet what he has ~90% odds of doing eventually, on top of a forward
  band that already prices the chance he never does. Robey was charged twice for the same risk.

  AMENDED: the probability at every level anchor is now CAREER-LEVEL P(EVER establishes | position
  x pick band). Tenure enters only as WINDOW MEMBERSHIP — who is counted in the denominator — never
  as a discount on the anchor. The level a player is compared to at a given tenure is still built
  from establishers' seasons AT THAT TENURE, so development shape is untouched.

  Everything else stands: the control build byte-reproduces the live board; the frozen year-zero
  surface is HELD; the store is unmoved; nothing merges.
