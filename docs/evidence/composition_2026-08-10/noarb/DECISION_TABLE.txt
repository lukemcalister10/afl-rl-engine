### THE FREE-MONEY CHECK — book appreciation per step vs the discount that variant charges
========================================================================================================
  For each step year N-1 -> N the book appreciates by  ratio[N]/ratio[N-1] - 1.  The variant
  charges a future discount at the rate its own schedule gives for the holder's CURRENT age at
  the start of the step. Typical draft age is 18.00 (MEASURED: median age_draft in every band),
  so at the start of step N-1 -> N the holder is 18 + (N-1).

  FREE MONEY = appreciation exceeds the discount charged: the book is expected to grow faster
  than the rate at which the engine discounts the future, so holding dominates and the price is
  an arbitrage against the engine's own discount. Flagged "ARB". This is a within-variant
  consistency test — each variant is judged against ITS OWN discount, never another's.
