### WHAT THIS TABLE DOES NOT CONTAIN
====================================================================================================================
  MIXES ARE NOT MEASURED. The menu names mixes (e.g. A-floor + V4) as candidates, and none is
  on this table because none has been emitted. Their effects are NOT additive and must not be
  read off by adding two rows: A-floor and the discount variants both act on the young end,
  and the per-item decomposition already shows this package's items interact. Any mix the
  sitting wants costs one emit (~2.5 min) and gets its own row, measured, not inferred.

  H IS ON THE TABLE BUT IS NOT A YEAR-1 CANDIDATE. The H rungs are shown because they were
  ordered and run, but the binding diagnostic (H_LADDER.txt) shows H reaches 0.2% of year-1
  cells and 11.0% of year-4 cells — it is a years-3-to-5 dial, and no value of H changes that.
  Its rows are here for completeness, not as live counterbalance options.

### CANDIDATE VERDICTS — three rows are CLOSED, one is open with a diagnosis
====================================================================================================================

  CLOSED — H RUNGS (H120/H125/H130). H is NOT a year-1 dial and no ceiling makes it one.
    Binding diagnostic: H reaches 0.2% of ND year-1 cells against 11.0% of year-4 cells, ~66x
    more often at year 4. ITEM C's release is evidence-weighted and year 1 is the year with no
    evidence. Across H 1.13->1.30 the year-1 book moved +0.0002. Not a sizing problem.

  CLOSED — A-AS-FLOOR. Zero ND year-1 movers, exactly as predicted BEFORE the emit.
    ITEM A cannot act at cohort year 1 by a mutual exclusion between its own two gates:
    reaching _a_blend requires ns>=1, which at year 1 forces gy/fE>=6, which pins
    LAM_SIT[6]=1.0, which makes A's share EXACTLY 0. The floor is max(e_full, b) and at s=0,
    b IS e_full. Correctly implemented at a site that is structurally silent at year 1.
    It does act from year 2 (60/96/76/52 cells at yr2-yr5).

  CLOSED — A-EVIDENCE-FADED DRAG. Same exclusion, same result: zero ND year-1 movers,
    matching the filed prediction. Moves yr2-yr5 (60/96/70/48), slightly gentler than the
    floor as designed. Both A variants are sound designs wired to a year-1-silent site.

  OPEN, WITH ITS PRE-REGISTRATION BREACHED AND THE CAUSE NAMED — V4.
    The pre-registration said the age-22 rate equals the 14% baseline, so year 4 would be
    unchanged. MEASURED: yr4 1.5310 -> 1.5637, +2.1%. The breach is real and is reported as
    one. THE DIAGNOSIS, with the candidate causes tested rather than assumed:

      (c) KEYING — RULED OUT BY DIRECT TEST. Calling the engine's own age_disc() in-process
          under mode 4 returns EXACTLY 0.14000 at ages 22.0, 22.5 and 23.0 — identical to
          LENS[bal]=0.14. The rate function is correct and IS current-age keyed.
      (b) DENOMINATOR — RULED OUT WITH A NUMBER. sum(v0) over the population moves +0.0044%,
          and only 1 of 1197 v0 rows moves at all. The frozen year-zero surface is intact and
          the ratio denominator is not the story.
      (a) MATURE TAIL — WRONG SIGN. Entrants aged 23+ at draft are 26-31 at yr4 and see
          15-16% under V4, which CUTS. It cannot produce a lift.

      THE ACTUAL CHANNEL — THE PEAK ESTIMATE. Restricting to rows whose CONTINUOUS age at
      year 4 lies in [22,23], where V4 charges exactly the baseline rate, 721 of 815 still
      moved. For those 815 rows:
          yr4 value uplift   median +2.459%   mean +2.390%
          peak field uplift  median +2.793%   mean +5.717%
      The peak uplift TRACKS the value uplift. The direct discounting of these players is
      unchanged; what moved is the PEAK ESTIMATE they are priced against, which is itself a
      forward-discounted object computed where V4 charges 11-13% rather than 14%. So the
      age-dynamic discount does not stay inside the year it is keyed to — it propagates
      through the peak estimate into later years.

      CONSEQUENCE: V4's year-4 number is NOT a clean "unchanged" baseline, and the peak/yr0
      and peak/yr1 columns for every discount variant should be read knowing the peak itself
      moved. This is REPORTED, NOT REPAIRED — changing where the peak estimate takes its
      discount is a wiring decision outside this act, and it is the owner's call.
