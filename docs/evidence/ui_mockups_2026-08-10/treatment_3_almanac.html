Gold is the cursor. It marks the row your pointer or keyboard is on, and nothing else — no figure, no control, no club is ever gold, so the eye can use it as a place-keeper down a list of 804.

Against the field puts every player on one rail: the left end is the free-hit value, the right end is the top of the board, and the two hairlines are where the 100th and the 300th player sit. It is square-root scaled, so a player ranked 400th is still a visible distance along instead of vanishing into the left edge. Rises and falls are signed and arrowed — colour is never the only signal.
