THE READING RULE, in plain words. A group is fairly priced if it appreciates between 0% and +14% over its first year. Below 0% is a SELL-SIDE RED — you could sell at draft day and buy back cheaper. Above +14% is a BUY-SIDE RED — you could buy at draft day and beat the cost of carrying him. Every breaching cell is scored on your own path test, and every raw year is printed so you can apply your own reading rather than this seat's. * thin cell · ** very thin.

A cell BREACHES when its year-1 appreciation exceeds +14%. For a breaching cell — limb (a) “the path afterwards does not keep beating carry” passes when NO year 2..7 sits above the carry line; limb (b) “the end destination does not keep increasing” passes when yr7 ≤ yr6 AND yr7 ≤ carry. The cell passes only when BOTH limbs pass.

THE MSD YEAR-1 EXCLUSION, AND ITS REASON. The cohort clock keys an MSD row on the DRAFT YEAR ITSELF, not draft year + 1 as it does for everyone else, because a mid-season draftee's first season IS his draft season.
