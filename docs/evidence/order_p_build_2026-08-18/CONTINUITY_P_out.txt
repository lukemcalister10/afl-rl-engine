   READ THIS PLAINLY. The object asks: for a row sitting exactly ON HIS BAR, does his price fall as
   career games step 0, 1, 2 ... 20? Under ORDER K the answer was no, for a row at his AGE bar.
   Under ORDER P a row at his AGE bar is NOT at his own bar if he was expensively drafted — he is
   below it by the whole pedigree premium — so the charge grows with his games and his price can
   fall. THAT IS THE MECHANISM THE OWNER ASKED FOR, working. Measured on the same rows, at their
   PEDIGREE bar, the price does not fall at all: T(0) clips to zero, so they pay nothing at any
   games count. Both numbers are printed above and neither is smoothed away.
