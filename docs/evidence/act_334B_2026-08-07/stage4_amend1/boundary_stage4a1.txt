  it is the mechanism keying on the CLAIM rather than on the games count:

    * this synthetic sweep REPLACES the record with a single season of g games @ 84.25, so e_full
      -- and therefore the surprise s -- GROWS WITH g. At one game the player is barely claiming
      anything above his anchor, so s is small and there is little to shrink. By two or three games

      population the owner pointed at.

      increasing). The dip lives entirely in s(g), which is the evidence talking, not the dial.

      is never worth less. It HOLDS on every probe on the amended arm, and the amendment introduces
