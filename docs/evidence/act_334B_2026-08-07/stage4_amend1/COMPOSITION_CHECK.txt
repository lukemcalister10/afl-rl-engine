================================================================================================================
#334 STAGE B / STAGE 4 AMENDMENT 1 — THE COMPOSITION CHECK (double-charging, and additive vs multiplicative)
================================================================================================================

(1) THE SHARED CHANNEL, MEASURED. Stage 4 and the amendment overlap in EXACTLY ONE place: the sit-out
    depth. It enters stage 4 through `sit` (a ratio of the retention surface) and it enters the
    amendment through R, which discounts the anchor that s is measured against. Pedigree (the pick
    axis) is NOT shared: it enters stage 4 only, and s does not read the pick at all except through R.

    Mraz, the calibration case:
      anchor UNDISCOUNTED (entry_anchor)          :     461.25
      retention R at his sit-out depth            :    0.45877
      anchor DISCOUNTED (R x entry_anchor)        :     211.61
      e_full                                      :    5068.48
      s against the DISCOUNTED anchor  (SHIPPED)  :   3.176066 nats
      s against the UNDISCOUNTED anchor           :   2.396857 nats
      => the sit-out discount contributes            0.779209 nats = 24.5% of s
      stage 4 separately charges the same sit-out through sit = 0.656601 (q = ped 0.209889 x sit = 0.137813)

    VERDICT ON DOUBLE-CHARGING: the overlap is REAL but it is NOT a double charge, for two reasons,
    and the second is the decisive one.
      (a) The two terms answer different questions about the same fact. `sit` asks "how much prior
          expectation survives the year he did not play"; R inside s asks "what is his prior price
          TODAY, which is what the evidence is actually contradicting". A sit-out lowers both the
          expectation AND the baseline the claim is measured from, and both are true.
      (b) THE ANCHOR CHOICE IS FORCED, not preferred. The blend is (1-lam)*anchor + lam*e_full.
          If s were measured against the UNDISCOUNTED V0, then s would be NON-ZERO at the exact point
          where lam cannot move the price at all (e_full == R*V0), and ZERO at a point where it can.
          The statistic would not be measuring the re-rate the mechanism performs. Measured against
          the discounted anchor, "s == 0" and "this change is inert" are THE SAME STATEMENT.
    So stage 4 is COMPOSED WITH, not replaced, and the sit-out prior effect is retained in both.

(2) ADDITIVE vs MULTIPLICATIVE, measured on the same landing.
    additive       : exponent = 1 + PED_BAR*(1-q) + W*s*u        [SHIPPED]
    multiplicative : exponent = (1 + PED_BAR*(1-q)) * (1 + W*s*u)  [the directive's offered form]

    On Mraz (lam raw 0.693727, stage-4 exponent 1.431093, s 3.176066, u 0.115239):
           W |   additive exp      price |   multipl. exp      price
         0.0 |       1.431093     3089.6 |       1.431093     3089.6
         1.0 |       1.797102     2729.0 |       1.954885     2587.9
         2.0 |       2.163110     2413.7 |       2.478677     2173.7
         3.0 |       2.529118     2137.8 |       3.002469     1831.7
         4.0 |       2.895126     1896.5 |       3.526261     1549.3
         5.0 |       3.261135     1685.5 |       4.050053     1316.1
         6.0 |       3.627143     1500.8 |       4.573845     1123.6
         8.0 |       4.359160     1198.1 |       5.621429      833.3

    The multiplicative form reaches the same Mraz landing at a smaller W (it scales the surprise demand
    BY the pedigree demand, 1.4311 on Mraz). The reason the ADDITIVE form ships is the shared channel in
    (1): multiplication charges the sit-out once through `sit`, once through R inside s, AND a third
    time through their PRODUCT. Addition charges it once per term and no more. The exponent is
    denominated in "passes of the lam ramp this record must clear", and two independent demands on the
    same evidence SUM -- a pick-1 player with a huge claim and a pick-60 player with no claim should
    each be charged their own demand, not each other's.

    THE CONSEQUENCE, and it is the honest cost of the choice: under the additive form a top-pick player
    with a big claim is charged the SAME surprise demand as a deep-pick player with the same claim, and
    only stage 4's own term separates them. The pedigree pair still separates (PROBES.md §(c)); it
    separates by stage 4's margin, not by a compounded one.
