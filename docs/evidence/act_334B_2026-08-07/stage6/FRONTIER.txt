1 · THE BINDING CONSTRAINT — the monotonicity law against the zero-cell bounds
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The correction's power to be ZERO WHERE ALREADY PRICED comes from its demonstrated-level axis, Sz
over z = log(e / entry_anchor): a player the engine has already re-rated upward reads a high z and
takes little or nothing.

But a steep DECREASING function of z is exactly what can invert prices: the shipped surface must
keep e*(1+delta) strictly increasing in e, or a strictly better career prices lower — the owner's
monotonicity law. Solving the two together (teach_log.txt, the joint block) the minimal declared
L-SMOOTH shrink of the Sz deviation is

    kappa = 0.912673      min d/dln(e)[e*(1+delta)] at rung 1.0 = +0.023790

(on the superseded estimand these were kappa = 0.693842 and +0.053337: the registered estimand
needs LESS shrink but leaves a THINNER monotonicity margin, because the correction it carries is
larger.)  The two laws remain in genuine tension; this is the frontier, not a defect.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

the shipped surface a MARGINAL (independence) product of factors that are in fact CORRELATED —
deep-ish picks tend also to carry low z and few games. The aggregate is protected by the single
declared conservation normaliser (Z = 0.772923, so |Z-1| = 0.227 IS the measured double-count), but
the CORNER is not:

    max |delta| the surface can produce at rung 1.0 = 1.8281  (nonKPP, pick 25, 6 games, z = -0.6)
    i.e. a +183% price move, against an aggregate the surface is calibrated to at +13.3%.

    louis-emmett       619 -> 1094   +76.7%   (pick 27, age 18, 6 career games)

6 · TWO ORDERING DEFECTS THIS BUILD FOUND IN ITSELF, ONE FIXED AND ONE DISCLOSED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) FIXED. The first cut ran the monotonicity law BEFORE the conservation normaliser, so the
    normaliser (which scales the base UP by 1/Z) re-broke the margin the law had just secured. The
    two are coupled and are now solved jointly. Caught by the probe suite, not by inspection.
(b) DISCLOSED, NOT FIXED, AND NOW LARGER. The pick-taper endpoint sweep that fixed the declared
    endpoints 34 -> 48 was read BEFORE the same normaliser.  Under the registered estimand the
    picks 41-64 band moves +0.708pp at rung 1.0 and +0.531pp at rung 0.75 against its 0.5pp bound
    (it met the bound at 0.75 on the superseded estimand).  It is met at rungs 0.25 and 0.5 (0.177 /
    0.354pp).  The endpoints were AGAIN NOT re-picked: re-picking a declared boundary once you can
    see which rung it strikes is tuning, and it is the same discipline the original build applied.
    The bound is carried as a live strike on rungs 0.75 and 1.0, which are struck anyway.
