------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE MSD CONVENTION USED HERE, AND WHY  (checked on the records before any number was aggregated)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. EVERY route uses the SAME grid. For an entrant labelled year Y, vpath slot N-1 is the value at
     season Y+N. Verified on all 106 MSD records: yrs[0] == Y+1 holds 106/106 times. No index shift is
     needed, and none was applied.

  2. The engine already folds the mid-season-draft season INTO the MSD year-1 measure. For every one
     of the 106 MSD records, games_yr1 == (games in season Y) + (games in season Y+1) -- 106/106. For ND
     it is season Y+1 alone. So an MSD's vpath[0] IS their year-1 value, and it already covers the
     season the owner points at. That is what "clock depth 1" means here.

  3. 57 of the 106 MSD records have their first playing season IN the label year itself. That is the
     owner's point restated by the data: the 2026 MSD played in 2026, so 2026 is their year 1, so they
     belong to the 2025 cohort.

  THE CONVENTION ADOPTED: depth alignment. Year N of a cohort takes each member's OWN year-N value --
  v0 for year 0, vpath[N-1] for year N -- for every route including MSD. Nothing is re-indexed and
  nothing is fabricated. The MSD entrants are simply filed into the cohort whose year 1 is theirs.

  THE ONE HONEST COST, stated plainly: the "has it reached year N" gate is still each entrant's own
  (label year + N <= 2026). An MSD sits one calendar year later than the rest of its cohort, so at the
  window edge the MSD leg drops out one year sooner. Concretely: the 2026 MSD -- the MSD leg of the
  2025 cohort -- has NO year-1 number inside the 2026 window (their grid year is 2027), so they are
  counted in the 2025 cohort but contribute nothing to its year-1 row yet. The MSD leg is therefore
  ALSO printed on its own below, so the owner can see exactly what it does and does not carry.

  READING IT, plainly:

  * ON THE AGGREGATE THE RULE CHANGES NOTHING AT YEAR 1 -- the move is exactly +0.000000, on the same 2517
    scoring entrants. That is not a coincidence and it is not a fudge. The only membership difference
    across the whole 2004-2025 book is the 18 MSD labelled 2026, who move IN (they are the 2025 cohort's
    MSD leg), and they have no year-1 number inside the window yet. Everyone else just changes which
    cohort they are filed under, and the aggregate does not care how the book is sorted. Membership
    does grow: the year-0 anchor row goes from 2517 entrants to 2535.

  * PER COHORT IT IS REAL, AND LARGE. Cohort 2023 moves +0.015633, cohort 2024 +0.014275, cohort 2025 -0.041302
    at the baseline. The MSD-leg rows show why: each cohort now takes the NEXT calendar MSD class, and
    those classes read very differently (1.352, 1.703, and nothing-yet at year 1). Under the old label
    grouping the 2025 cohort was carrying the 2025 MSD at 1.703 -- the inflated pool figure the ruling
    struck. Under the rule it carries the 2026 MSD instead, who have not scored yet, and the 2025
    cohort reads 0.9143 rather than 0.9556.
