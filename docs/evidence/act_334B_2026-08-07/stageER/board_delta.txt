EVERY ONE OF THE 28 MOVERS IS A KEY-POSITION FORWARD (KPF).
Mechanism, named: the era multiplier's one high-leverage site on the shipped value
path was _kpf_LD(p,Y) (_merged_recover.py:899/904 pre-strip) — the KPF 'level
demonstrated', the mean of the best two era-adjusted season averages in the recent
window. That LD feeds the W4 KPF credit regime (dm = max(m, LD-REPL), kpfup(dm)),
which is where the engine's KPF-specific reward multiplier is decided, so a small
shift in LD is levered into a visible price move. The other live sites — bestlvl()
(a MAX over seasons, so a ~1.0 rescale rarely changes which season wins) and
_staleness_grade() (a narrow, mostly-saturated interpolation) — produced 0 movers
of their own.

The effect is monotone in age: the OLDER the bucket, the harder it is cut. That is
the era table's own shape read back to us — a long career reaches back into the
2013-2020 years the table was DEFLATING (factor <1) and the 2009-2012 / 2021-2025
years it was INFLATING (factor >1); the removal lands hardest where the recent
window sits in the inflating tail. The <=22 bucket is essentially untouched
(1 mover, 0.0011%% mean move) — young players have little or no qualifying history
for the multiplier to have acted on.
