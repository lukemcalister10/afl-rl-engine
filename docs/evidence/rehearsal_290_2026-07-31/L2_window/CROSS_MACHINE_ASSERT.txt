WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES

 1. The engine's COMPUTE path is cross-CPU deterministic to the byte when the frozen
    surface is supplied. The determinism work (item 380/381 + the order-fixed math.fsum
    closed form in par_build.loclin) holds on a real two-machine test, not just the
    one-box forced-ISA test its note records.

 2. The ONLY cross-machine sensitivity found anywhere in this chain is in the FITTING of
    v0surf. That is precisely the act the freeze exists to remove.

 3. Therefore the ruling is validated by measurement: with data/v0surf.pkl committed as
    bytes, L3-L8, L6's convergence and the landing are reproducible on any machine. The
    gap was never the engine; it was that the artifact was not carried.

 4. It retro-confirms the attribution of G-Y0 19.869% vs 13.919% to the surface bytes,
    since every other input is now PROVEN CPU-invariant rather than assumed to be.
