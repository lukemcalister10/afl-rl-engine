C · WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE
--------------------------------------
  SETTLED   : on THIS box, with the pinned stack (py 3.12.3 / numpy 2.4.4 / scipy 1.17.1 /
              sklearn 1.8.0), the peak build and the band refit are byte-reproducible run to run.
  NOT SETTLED: the CROSS-MACHINE question. The band is a RandomForest and q97m a GBR, both
              through numpy's OpenBLAS, which is DYNAMIC_ARCH — the exact mechanism that made
              v0surf machine-sensitive (N16) and q97m machine-sensitive before its freeze.
              On-box determinism is not cross-machine determinism; the v0surf episode is the
              standing proof of that distinction (19.869% vs 13.919% on one artifact's bytes).
              This container ALSO migrated hosts mid-session once already (v552 provenance).

  So the measurement here narrows the question; it does not answer it, and nothing above is a
  clearance to refit-and-ship. The remedy already ruled for v0surf — fit once, freeze the bytes,
  travel them, name the md5 in every figure — is the candidate remedy for all three. That is a
  seam call and is NOT taken here.
