## 1 · The defect this order fixes

Order 31 lawfully deleted the pick-prior par tables — the owner's ruled forbidden set, aimed at
the PICK axis. Those tables carried TWO axes: pick AND development stage. Both were deleted
together. The two normalization denominators that survived in the production leg (the places the
engine divides shown output by "the bar") were re-referenced to the FLAT positional bars a MATURE
player must clear (KPD 65.4 · KPF 63.8 · MID 77.1 · RUCK 75.5 · SD 75.3 · SF 67.9). S1 measured
that those bars fail 86–100% of age-18/19 seasons even for players who turn out fine. So since
Order 31, young output has been judged against mature standards inside the production core:

- **Site 1 — the evidence weight.** `Q = clip(career average / bar, 0, 2)` — the weight that
  releases the production ceiling. Its one live consumer under this law is the RUCK ceiling
  release, so the flat bar was suppressing exactly the young-ruck production credit (RUCK −0.43
  was the worst positional cut in the repair's attribution).
- **Site 2 — the decay gate.** `pr = best demonstrated level / bar`; a long-tenured row with
  `pr < 0.55` is classed "mediocre-for-years" and crushed toward a fraction of entry value.
  Judged against the mature bar, a decent-for-his-age fifth-year 22-year-old is mis-classed.

## 3 · The re-derivation (no double-paying age)

The repair's re-mix constants (κ, γ_u, η, γ_d) and its age credit were calibrated against engine

**The mature gate did what the prereg predicted (D-1):** of 3,960 knob points, exactly ONE
passes — the repaired point itself. The re-mix knobs are global in games, so ANY movement

points; κ±.02 → 4.3 points on marcus-herbert, η±.03 → 9.6 on billy-cootee). The four knobs
therefore stay at κ=.24, γ_u=11, η=.41, γ_d=14 — pinned by the owner's own core identity
control, demonstrated numerically inside the derivation, not assumed.

**The credit scale:** feasible α runs [0.75, 1.00] on the pinned knobs — the 1.14 no-arb line

31.3 against 33.0 at α=1): the corrected surface says the repair's credit was over-paying the
1–9-game cells at the margin. In plain words: a quarter of the age credit was compensation for
the blind denominators, and with the denominators fixed it comes off.

**The diagnostic the discipline requires:** without the mature gate, the grid's unconstrained
