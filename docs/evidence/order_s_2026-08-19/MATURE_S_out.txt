5 · WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE CHARGE — THE BAR MOVEMENT ON MATURE ROWS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The charge reads BAR = o32_gate_bar(pos, age) + PG(ln v0, class). On a mature row FIX B1 makes
   it read the YOUNG PG. Swapping in the mature PG moves the bar by exactly the gap above, and
   the surplus by minus that. A LOWER mature premium => a LOWER bar => a HIGHER surplus =>
   a SMALLER charge.

   MAX |gap| inside the prereg materiality window (v0 184-1658): 4.025 points a game
   MAX |gap| anywhere on the printed grid                      : 7.981 points a game
   S5-F1 bar: <= 1.0 point a game everywhere in the window would make the domain concern a NULL.
   S5-F1: does NOT fire — the surfaces differ materially

   S5-F2 (mature grid not monotone after isotonisation): does not fire

   THE WATCHED SHAPE (never a target): mature rows ABOVE their age bar but BELOW the pedigree bar

     their mean shortfall against the YOUNG premium bar : -5.020 pts a game
