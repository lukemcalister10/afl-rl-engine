1 · HOW MANY ROWS ARE PARKED AT THE CAP, AND HOW FAR APART THEIR RECORDS ARE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   A row is PARKED when its surplus is at or below the cap crossing, s_cross = s0 - (TMAX-1)/THETA_R.
   Parked rows are TIED in T under a hard clip: their per-game records no longer separate them at
   all, and only games do. THAT IS THE DEFECT THE OWNER NAMED.

   anchor         TMAX    s_cross   parked n    their s_ped spread tied away

   p15         13.9489    -22.149         64   -70.1..-22.2  47.9 pts a game
   p20         11.8950    -19.025         97   -70.1..-19.1  51.0 pts a game

   READ THE p20 ROW. At the anchor the OWNER HIMSELF ASKED FOR, 97 charged rows are parked and
   their per-game records span 51.0 points a game. Under the hard clip every one of them pays the
   IDENTICAL rate. LOWERING THE CAP MAKES THE DEFECT HE NAMED WORSE, not better — 97 rows tied at
   p20 against 12 at ORDER P's p5. THAT IS WHY HE ASKED FOR A COMPRESSION INSTEAD OF A LOWER CAP,
   and it is the single strongest argument in this section. UNDER THE COMPRESSION NO ROW IS TIED:
   T'(s) is strictly increasing in shortfall everywhere and that is asserted in the engine at load
   (S-S4), on a dense sweep, on every board that carries the form.

2 · PAIRWISE, AT EQUAL GAMES — DOES WORSE PLAY ALWAYS COST MORE?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Every pair of charged rows whose CAREER GAMES agree to within 1, so A(g) is held fixed and the
   only thing left is the record. A pair INVERTS when the row with the WORSE surplus is charged
   the SAME or LESS. Ties count as inversions: the owner's requirement is "at least slightly more".

   ONE EXCLUSION, AND IT IS NECESSARY RATHER THAN CONVENIENT: pairs in which BOTH rows are charged
   NOTHING are dropped. Both sit above the zero clip — they are producing at or above what their
   entry price implies — and a tie at zero charge is the mechanism working, not the cap defect.
   THIS SEAT's FIRST RUN DID NOT EXCLUDE THEM and reported 475 "ties" on a compression board,
   which was the zero-clip region being counted as the cap. That was the scorer, not the form.
