## NOTES

### N1 — The two hard blockers to installing the ruled curve
1. **`data/v0surf.pkl` is curve-keyed and load-or-HALT.** `_v0surf_sig` hashes `_PVC0` itself
   (`_merged_recover.py:1289`). Swap in payload `e69a3f38` and the signature changes, the frozen set no longer
   contains it, and `:1359` raises `v0surf FROZEN-SIGNATURE HALT` — by design, the silent-refit fallback was
   deleted on 2026-07-28. The re-bake must ride in the same commit
   (`RL_V0SURF_REFIT=1 RL_BAKE_V0SURF=1 refit_v0surf.py --bake`, then re-pin `expected_boot.v0surf`).
   Note the surface must be frozen for **both** `_PVC0` states (L1b transient + ruled terminal), or the L1b
   pass will fit live again — the exact defect `refit_v0surf.py:56-60` records having already happened once.
2. **The hard pick-1 pin is asserted three times.** `rl_model.py:916` (`assert nd[1]==3000` inside the shared
   `_split_ladder`), `rl_export.py:145`, and the unconditional `NUMÉRAIRE HALT` at `rl_export.py:163-167`. A
   pooled-numeraire curve will not boot unless its pick-1 is coincidentally 3000, or these are re-scoped.

### N2 — SCAR is not merely a default, it is pinned five deep
`RL_GAMMA=0.85` is the code default (`rl_model.py:504`), the manifest pin (`data/model_config.json` `vars`),
the panel export (`run_panel.sh:26`), a `setdefault` in four forward-valuation modules, and a **hard
assignment** (`os.environ['RL_GAMMA']='0.85'`) in `build_peak_model_v4.py:8`. Moving to VOR γ=1.0 changes
`val()` and `SCALE` for every player and therefore invalidates rows 5, 10, 21, 22, 23, 39 and 49 in one step.
`pvc_curve_v2.json:104` explicitly declares itself *"SCAR-denominated BY CONSTRUCTION"* and says a natively-VOR
curve *"would be a referee-era question"*.

### N3 — The frozen fitted artifacts carry no provenance stamp
`cm_400.pkl`, `q97m.pkl`, `peak_model_v4.pkl`, `pvc_snapshot.json`, `bust_prior_table.json` are pinned by md5 in
`data/expected_boot.json` but **none records the store or curve it was trained on**. Their staleness is
established structurally (they are fit off `MA.data` and `MA.PVC`) and by commit date (2026-07-02 seed /
2026-07-14 q97m freeze), not by a stamp. This is itself a gap worth closing at the re-derivation: the
`_fv_note` pattern (a source-set identity asserted by Guard 5) exists for the FV *source*, but nothing binds a
fitted *artifact* to its training store.

### N4 — Axis skew introduced by ITEM 271 and not carried into the frozen models
`rl_model.py` migrated the read axis from the drafted position to `gfut` at every site (`:339, 341, 405, 426,
451, 471, 646, 664, 674, 677, 1134, 1136`). The frozen models were trained on the **old** axis:
`build_peak_model_v4.py:30, 40` use `MA.GRP[p['pos']]`. `cm_400` and `q97m` were trained through
`conditional_prior._feat` which *does* use `gfut` (`:121`) — but on a pre-271 store where the two coincided.
Either way, the frozen models see a feature distribution the live engine no longer produces.

### N5 — Ruled "per-season par teaching" vs what is live
`par_build.gather()` produces one observation per (player, tenure) at `pos = MA.gfut(p)` — a single
whole-career position stamped onto every season of that player. The ruled basis wants the season's own bar.
