## NOTES

### N1 — the FROZEN-RULER pin block contains its own contradiction
`one_source_selftest.py:490-500` carries three pins. The block's comment (`:495-498`) correctly explains that the #274 mop-up moved only `_contract_md5`, and then states as a standing rule: *"Curve SOURCE store and per_entrant are UNCHANGED below - nothing was re-derived, so those two pins must NOT move."* That sentence is true of the act it describes and false of the next one. The #279 re-derivation moves the payload (`08ea9375 → e69a3f38`), the derivation store, and the per-entrant file — so all three pins move, plus `pool_value`, plus the file md5, plus `contract_sha256`. The instruction sitting next to the constants is the thing an adoption commit will read first.

### N2 — the pool sibling triplet, measured
`299` lives in exactly three places on the live path:
1. `engine/rl_after/pvc_curve_v2.json` → `pool_value: 299` (the artifact of record; self-documents `stamp.prev_pool_value: 528`)
2. `ui/release_pick_curve.json` → `pool_value: 299` (the mirror; its md5 is pinned at `one_source_selftest.py:490`)

### N3 — the sealed F5 layer is the load-bearing stale number, not the curve
The curve identity is *supposed* to move at adoption; the machinery for that exists and is documented. What has no such machinery is the pair `entrant_layer_pvc: 77611` / `f5_draft_pvc: 68556`, sealed at `a17aafed` (`session_2026-07-18/legf5/sealed_entrant_structure.json`) and pinned in `data/release_contract.json f5_entrant_reconciliation` (F5) and rendered in the UI (A6). So the equality is algebraically self-fulfilling and will still print `reconciled: true` after the ladder falls to 54,722 — while the "national-draft deep-tail residual" the board displays (`board.js:658-659`) goes from 2,631 to roughly 13,834, i.e. from 3% to 18% of the entrant layer. A gate that cannot fail, showing a number that has silently changed meaning.

### N4 — FHV is a two-file constant, and its rationale is stale in two directions

Separately, the rationale block that justifies 190 is stale on both of its comparators: it cites `528 (pool_value)` (C2) and it advertises the per-window upgrade schedule `MSD ~352 / SSP ~277 / OTHER ~97` (C3) against #279's measured `MSD 296.4 / SSP 333.4 / POOL 234.3` — where the MSD/SSP ordering inverts.

### N5 — the currency the UI names is the one the ruling superseded
