7 · WHAT A LEVEL OFFSET IS WORTH — THE TRANSLATION WRITTEN DOWN IN THE PREREG
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   In the unclipped region  d ln(retained pedigree)/ds = LAMBDA*A(g)*THETA_R = BETA_sat*A(g).
   So a level offset of x points a game moves the RETAINED pedigree leg by exp(BETA_sat*A(g)*x)-1.
   BETA_sat = 0.114646. A(g) = 1 - exp(-g/9.890).
