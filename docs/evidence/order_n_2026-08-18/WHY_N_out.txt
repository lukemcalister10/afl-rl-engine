ORDER N — WHY THE TWO RAILS DO NOT OVERLAP
======================================================================================================================

THE SHORT VERSION. The young players who are ABOVE their age bar are, very largely, the young
players taken at the TOP of the draft. So a charge that relieves over-performers relieves the top
of the draft, and the top of the draft is exactly where the +14%% buy rail has almost no headroom.
The conflict is between the owner's fix and the no-arbitrage rail, and it runs through one
population fact. Here is the fact.
