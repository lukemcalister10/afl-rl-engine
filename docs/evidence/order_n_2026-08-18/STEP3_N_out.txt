2 · THE TILT, AND TWO DEVIATIONS FROM THE PREREGISTERED FORM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   PREREG_N.md section 5 declared:   pi *= max(0, 1 - ETA_N * A(g) * exp(-THETA*(s - s0)) )
   What is derived here instead is:  pi *= exp( -LAMBDA * A(g) * T(s) ),  T(s) = clip(1 - THETA_R*(s - s0), 0, TMAX)

   TWO CHANGES. Both are disclosed, both were forced by the measurement, and neither is cosmetic.

   DEVIATION D1 — exp(-X) in place of (1 - X).
   exp(-X) lies in (0, 1] for every X >= 0. The multiplier can therefore never go negative and the
   max(0, .) clamp the current mechanism needs disappears from the law. Structural property N-S4
   stops being a thing to check and becomes a thing that cannot fail.

   DEVIATION D2 — the tilt is LINEAR in surplus inside the exponent, not exponential.
   This one matters and here is the number that forced it. With the preregistered exponential tilt
   at the measured THETA, the multiplier exp(-THETA*(s - s0)) spans the cohort from 0.031 to 175.8 —
   four orders of magnitude. Worse, it makes the DELIVERED slope depend on where a row sits: the
   charge would lean 350 times harder on a row at the bottom of the spread than on one at the top,
   which is not what Step 2 measured. Step 2 measured ONE slope, BETA(g), the same at every s.

   With the linear-in-s tilt the delivered slope is exactly constant:

       d ln(retained pedigree) / ds  =  LAMBDA * A(g) * THETA_R

   So setting  LAMBDA * THETA_R = BETA_sat  makes the pedigree leg respond to performance surplus
   at exactly the rate the outcome data says it should, at every level of s, and the response
   scales with A(g) — which reproduces the measured BETA(g) = BETA_sat * A(g) exactly.

       LAMBDA * THETA_R = BETA_sat = 0.11521      90% CI [0.10434, 0.12828]

   That is ONE equation. LAMBDA is fixed by the anchoring identity in Step 4, and THETA_R follows
   as BETA_sat / LAMBDA. There is no free parameter left and nothing to tune.

3 · s0 AND THE CAP
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   s0 = the GAMES-WEIGHTED mean performance surplus of the young cohort = +2.3163 points per game.
   Weighting by games is deliberate: a two-game season must not set the centre of the scale.
   Unweighted mean -4.4476, median -3.8795, p5 -31.72, p95 +19.19.
   T(s0) = 1 by construction: a row producing exactly at the cohort centre pays the base charge.

   THE CAP. T is clipped below at 0 and above at TMAX. TMAX is set at the value T takes for a row
   at the cohort's own 5th percentile of surplus (-31.72 points per game), so the worst-producing
   5%% of the cohort all pay the same top rate rather than an unbounded one. That bound is the
   data's own spread; no number here was picked by hand. TMAX is computed in Step 4, where
   THETA_R becomes known.

   THE LOWER CLIP AT ZERO IS THE HEADLINE OF THE WHOLE MECHANISM, so it is stated plainly:
   a young player far enough above his age bar pays NO charge on his pedigree leg at all. He keeps
   the whole prior. The surplus at which that happens is s0 + 1/THETA_R and it is reported in Step 4.

4 · THE PREREGISTERED STRUCTURAL PROPERTIES, CHECKED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   N-S1  A(0) = 0 exactly, so pi(0) = D and no day-0 print can move                                     PASS
   N-S2  A is non-decreasing in g on [0, 400]                                                           PASS
   N-S3  T is non-increasing in s -- structural: T is a clipped decreasing linear function              PASS
   N-S4  exp(-LAMBDA*A*T) is in (0, 1] for every non-negative LAMBDA, A, T -- STRUCTURAL                PASS

   N-S5 (rows with no age-bar content) is settled as follows and stated plainly: the age bar is
   FLAT from age 24, so for a row at 24 or older the surplus s is his distance from the mature bar.
   That needs no special case, and it is the right reading: an old row far below the mature bar
   SHOULD lose more of his pedigree prior, and one above it should keep more. The C3 offsets go to
   zero on their own, which is the engine's own cap law.
