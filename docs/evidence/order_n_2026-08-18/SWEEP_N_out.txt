   THIS IS THE ANSWER TO REQUIREMENT (c), AND IT IS NOT THE ONE THE ORDER ASSUMED.
   The retained prior under the CURRENT mechanism is NOT monotone: it recovers as games rise.

   That does NOT make the current mechanism fine. It makes the complaint precise. A 36-game player
   does not end up holding more prior than a 17-game player. What he does is pay a SMALLER SHARE of
   it, at exactly the point where the evidence about him is strongest. That is the defect.
