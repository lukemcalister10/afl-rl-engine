*** THE FINDING THAT REPLACES THE HYPOTHESIS ***
  For a grace-eligible entrant (entry age <= 19 -- the overwhelming majority of the draft), the
  entry price and the year-1 price weight every surviving season IDENTICALLY (accretion 1.00000).
  The grace-A rule gives seasons 1 and 2 full weight at the entry clock and exactly one extra free
  step at the engine clock, and the two were DESIGNED to line up.  The consequence, which appears
  not to have been carried into the no-arb benchmark, is that THE ENTRY->YEAR-1 STEP EARNS NO CARRY
  FOR A GRACE-ELIGIBLE ENTRANT.  His board-consistent fair year-1 mark is
        fair_grace = 1.00 x (1 - s1)        NOT   fair_C = 1.14 x (1 - s1).
  For a mature-age entrant (>= 20) there is no grace on either clock, the step accretes by 1.30,
  and Order C's fair_C = 1.14 x (1 - s1) is exactly right for him.
