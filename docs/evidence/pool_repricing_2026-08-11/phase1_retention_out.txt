======================================================================================================================
THE MEAN-PRESERVING CHECK -- the owner's law, CHECKED rather than asserted
======================================================================================================================

  The law (D8 amendment): once a pathway's entry price is calibrated to that pathway's own
  realized returns -- SITTERS INCLUDED -- any within-pathway sitter differential must be a
  REDISTRIBUTION and never a net charge. So if sitters carry retention R < 1, the non-sitters of
  that same pathway must carry an uplift U > 1 with the entry-weighted mean landing on 1.0000:

      U  =  ( SUM_all e  -  SUM_sitters e*R )  /  SUM_non-sitters e
