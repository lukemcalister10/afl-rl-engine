6 · WOULD A PER-POSITION PREMIUM REDUCE OR WORSEN THE PICK REVERSAL?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The reversal is dPG/dln(v0) > 1/(BETA_sat*A). Pooling AVERAGES slopes. A split replaces the
   pooled slope with each position's own. Where a position's own slope is STEEPER than the pool,
   splitting makes its reversal WORSE; where it is shallower, splitting makes it better.

   pos       own slope pool slope   own-pool | effect of splitting on the reversal     
   KPD          3.0467     8.2896    -5.2429 | better — its own slope is shallower     
   KPF          8.6718     8.2896    +0.3822 | WORSE — its own slope is steeper than the pool
   RUCK         8.9213     8.2896    +0.6317 | WORSE — its own slope is steeper than the pool
   MID          9.9129     8.9432    +0.9697 | WORSE — its own slope is steeper than the pool
   SD           9.0224     8.9432    +0.0792 | no material change — its slope is the pool's
