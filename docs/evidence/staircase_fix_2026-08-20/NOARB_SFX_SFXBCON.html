THE MSD YEAR-1 EXCLUSION, AND ITS REASON. The cohort clock keys an MSD row on the DRAFT YEAR ITSELF, not draft year + 1 as it does for everyone else, because a mid-season draftee's first season IS his draft season. At year 1 an MSD row therefore falls before the first year his path covers. Those rows are counted PRE-WINDOW and EXCLUDED from the year-1 cell rather than scored as zero — scoring them as zero would invent a collapse that did not happen. That, and only that, is why MSD's yr1 cell reads “—”, why MSD carries no year-1 verdict on either board, and why MSD can carry no verdict CHANGE either.
