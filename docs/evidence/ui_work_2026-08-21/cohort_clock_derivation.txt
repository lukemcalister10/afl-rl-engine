READING: MSD is the ONLY entry type whose modal gap is 0 — a mid-season-draft selection debuts in
the SAME calendar year as his draft year. Every other type debuts the year AFTER, because the
national, rookie and pre-season/supplemental drafts happen at the END of a year for the season
that follows. So a 2024 ND draftee and a 2025 MSD selection both first take the field in 2025:
one intake, two doors, labelled by the national-draft year.
