THE ONE PLACE A 14-18 GAME SEASON IS STILL DISCOUNTED — and it is MEASURED, not a norm
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   rho31(g), the production-reliability weight, is still climbing at 15-18 games (it reaches
   ~91-95%% of its 22-game value). But rho31 is FITTED ON RAW GAMES with no season length in it:
     rho31(g) = 1 - exp(-(g/29.19)^0.8015)   + the measured re-mix bump
   Its tau is 29.19 GAMES — larger than any season length. It is a career-evidence curve, not a
   season-completeness ratio. Rebasing "a season" to 18 would not touch it; only a refit would,
   and the refit would have to be justified by out-of-sample error, not by a convention.
