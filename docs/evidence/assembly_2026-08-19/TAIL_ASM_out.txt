WHY THEY DIFFER, SAID PLAINLY. The candidate is softer than F5's convergence point on one axis
and stiffer on another. Its slope 0.105 is ABOVE the CI floor 0.10416 F5 priced, which charges
the deep cell MORE; its cap is the SMOOTH compression rather than a hard clip, and the
compression sits strictly BELOW the clip ceiling everywhere, which charges the deep cell LESS.
The two do not cancel exactly and there was never a reason to expect them to.

  F5's caveat stands unchanged and is repeated here rather than left behind: the deep cell is

games — is carried forward, not closed.
