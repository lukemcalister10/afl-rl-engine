*** RUCK'S MISFIRE IS THE C3 AGE-DELTA OBJECT, NOT PG. ***
    The residual is age-shaped with an interval that excludes zero, and an age column removes
    materially more of it than a constant does. A LEVEL OFFSET IS THE WRONG REPAIR: it would
    fit the average of a swing and be wrong at BOTH ends — too generous to a 23-year-old ruck
    and still too harsh on a 21-year-old.
    PER THE CHARTER: NO PREMIUM OFFSET IS WIRED FOR RUCK. The finding is reported with its
    evidence and the C3 object is named as the place a future order would work.

SD IS THE CONTROL AND IT BEHAVES: its age slope includes zero, so the test CAN tell flat from sloped, and SD reads flat.

WHAT THIS DIAGNOSIS DOES NOT CLAIM. It does not fit a replacement C3 column and it does not price
one. It answers the one question the charter asked — WHICH OBJECT — and stops. The RUCK cells at
ages 19 and 20 are thin and are not read. RUCK holds the widest pooled interval of the six
positions (T1), and nothing here narrows it.
