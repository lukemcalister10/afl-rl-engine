  The ramp is a DEPTH convention: it prorates the in-progress season's contribution to an absence
  clock concavely (f**1.5) instead of linearly, so early-season absence counts for less and
  late-season absence for more. It is the engine's OWN already-ruled shape, used at two existing
  sites for exactly this question. It is deliberately NOT applied to the I1 credit, which is a
  participation weight — the engine's own comment at the D12 site gives that reason and it stands.
