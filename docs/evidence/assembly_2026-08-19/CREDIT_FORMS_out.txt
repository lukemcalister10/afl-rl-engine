HOW THE RAW READING WAS MADE MONOTONE, AND WHAT WAS NOT INVENTED.
  The raw cells INVERT at g = 3, 7 and 10 — a 3-game season would credit LESS than a 2-game one,
  which is exactly what the guard exists to remove. The raw variant is monotonised by RUNNING
  MAXIMUM, deliberately NOT by the pool-adjacent-violators guard: PAVA AVERAGES the violating
  cells and therefore produces numbers that are not any measured cell (the guarded 0.23834 is a
  pooled value, not an F1 reading). A running maximum carries only MEASURED CELL VALUES forward.
  THE ONE NON-CELL NUMBER IS THE CAP AT 1.0 at g=9, where the raw cell reads 1.03200. That cap is
  STRUCTURAL AND PRE-EXISTING — the wired law and the charter both cap a season at one full played
  season — so it is applied, not invented. Nothing else was chosen.

  READ THE TWO COLUMNS: the raw reading is UNIFORMLY MORE GENEROUS from 2 games up. At 2 games it
  credits 0.471 against the guarded 0.238; at 5 games 0.471 against 0.246; at 9 games 1.000
  against 0.888. The guarded curve is the harsher of the two everywhere they differ.

THE MID-SEASON TIMING CENSUS — A CENSUS OF EXPOSURE, NOT A PROJECTION OF ANY SEASON
==========================================================================================================================
THE ISSUE, STATED PLAINLY. F1 measured this curve on COMPLETED seasons. The engine applies it to
the 2026 season while that season is IN PROGRESS, at fE = 0.92. A first-year who has played a few
games so far is being credited as though a few games were his whole season. If he goes on to play
more, his credit rises steeply — the curve is at its steepest exactly where these rows sit.
NOTHING IS PROJECTED BELOW. The census asks only: how many I1-moved rows are exposed to this, and
how much would their credit move if the same row finished on a higher games count.
