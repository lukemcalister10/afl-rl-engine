3. THE FIVE LARGEST MOVEMENTS, with causes  (ranked on CTRL -> NEW, the isolated #338 effect)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  #1  PICKS 21-64, YEAR 1 mean +37.1 (492.4 -> 529.5); ratio 1.007 -> 1.083, and against the
      shipped OLD table 0.9933 -> 1.083, +9.0 ratio points.
      CAUSE: the 2-season minimum on ND 41+ and the 3-season minimum on ND 21-40. Under the old
      rule a late pick the source DB kept no rows for reconstructed as DELISTED on draft day and
      priced at the 2% remnant in his own year 1. He now prices as a LISTED sitting-out player.
      The single biggest number on this sheet, and the one the owner's complaint points at.

  #2  ALL PICKS, YEAR 2 "ended" ZEROS 154 -> 0 (159 -> 0 against the shipped OLD table).
      CAUSE: the emitted window. Every one of those rows was a career the table declared OVER at
      year 2 because the emitter stopped at the last PLAYED year and there were no played years.
      Under #338 no drafted player's window can close before the end of his listed tenure, so at
      year 2 the cohort now contains ZERO concluded careers. Not one career was invented: the
      zeros move LATER (year 3: 212 -> 141; year 4: 271 -> 258), they do not disappear.

  #3  PICKS 21-64, YEAR 2 mean +32.5 (647.7 -> 680.2); ratio 1.325 -> 1.392. Zeros 149 -> 0.
      CAUSE: same rule, second tenure year. This is where the 2-season ND 41+ minimum lands.

  #4  PICKS 21-64, YEAR 3 mean +20.5 (736.1 -> 756.6); ratio 1.506 -> 1.548. Zeros 207 -> 141.
      CAUSE: the 3-season ND 21-40 minimum, which reaches exactly one year further than the
      41+ band's. The 66-row zero drop is the 21-40 evidence-less cohort's third listed season.

  #5  PICKS 1-20, YEAR 4 mean +15.0 (2538.9 -> 2553.9); zeros 13 -> 0.
      CAUSE: the 4-season ND 1-20 minimum, the rule's longest reach and its smallest population
      (4 evidence-less records in the band, plus short real careers extended to the minimum).
      It is the LAST row #338 can touch — years 5+ are identical by construction.

  (Runner-up, for completeness: ALL PICKS YEAR 1 mean +26.7, ratio 1.091 -> 1.123. It is the
   weighted sum of #1 and the small 1-20 move, so it is not listed as a separate mover.)

4. THE "USELESS" ASYMMETRY — historical vs current valuation. IS IT VISIBLY REDUCED?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
YES, and at the sharpest test it is ELIMINATED, not merely reduced. Three readings, in the
table's own terms, honest about which one moves and which one does not:

  (a) ERA PARITY AT MATCHED IDENTITY — the decisive one. Two players, same route, same pick,
      same position, different eras, in THIS per_entrant lineage:
          scott-simpson  ND pick 44 KPF entry 2007 : year-1 value    7  ->  216
          isaac-keeler   ND pick 44 KPF entry 2022 : year-1 value  216  ->  216   (unmoved)
          james-condos   RD pool     MID entry 2003 : year-1 value    9  ->  170
          zane-trew      RD pool     MID entry 2020 : year-1 value  170  ->  170   (unmoved)
      The historical record was worth 3% of his current-era twin. He is now worth EXACTLY the
      same number, to the point. The current-era player did not move at all. This is the
      asymmetry the owner called useless, and on this test it is gone — the two eras now price
      through one code path. (Same pair, same result, as the book's own P5 at commit 30996f8.)

  (b) THE EVIDENCE-LESS COHORT INSIDE THE MEASURED 1,197 (133 records, all no-data careers):
          mean YEAR-1 value        8.1  ->  248.6      (x30.7)
          mean emitted window     1.00  ->  2.31 years
      They were being carried in the denominator at effectively zero for reasons of missing
      data, not of football. 182 of the 1,197 gain a longer window in total (103 in picks 41-64,
      66 in 21-40, 13 in 1-20; 81 historical classes and 101 current ones).

  (c) THE PICK-BAND GAP AT YEAR 1, the asymmetry as the shipped table itself displayed it:
          picks 1-20 ratio  1.1473 -> 1.150      gap 1-20 minus 21-64:
          picks 21-64 ratio 0.9933 -> 1.083          0.154  ->  0.067   (-56%)
      The old table said a late-round pick was worth LESS one year after being drafted than on
      draft day (0.993x) while a top-20 pick gained 15%. That specific absurdity is gone: every
      band now gains in year 1, and the spread between the bands has more than halved.

  WHERE IT DOES *NOT* VISIBLY MOVE, stated plainly so the sheet is not oversold: split by class
  ERA rather than by pick band, the year-1 ratio gap barely changes —
          2004-2012 half : 1.079 -> 1.109        2013-2022 half : 1.102 -> 1.136
          gap (current minus historical) : 0.023 -> 0.027
  The era halves each contain both data-rich and data-poor records, so the correction lifts both
  by a similar amount and the AGGREGATE era ratio is not where the defect was visible. The defect
  was never "historical classes read low on average"; it was "a historical record with no rows
  reads at the 2% remnant while its current twin reads at full value", which is (a), and (a) is
  closed. Do not quote the era-half gap as evidence either way.
