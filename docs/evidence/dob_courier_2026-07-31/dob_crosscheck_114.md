## Method / caveats

- All 114 sheet dates match Draftguru exactly. The uniformity suggests the sheet was very likely populated from Draftguru, so Draftguru alone is not an independent check. That is why a second source was pulled for every row rather than a sample.
- 111 of 114 rows carry an independent second-source confirmation. 3 rows (Luke Taylor #37, Jake Furfaro #38, Danny Hughes #102) have no reachable second source: Footyinfo has no profile for Furfaro, and for Taylor and Hughes it indexes only an older same-named player. Draftguru's draft year/club/pick match the sheet in all three cases, so the identification is sound, but the date rests on one database.
- Two rows have a genuine two-way conflict between reputable databases (rows 60 and 107). No third source could be located for either, so neither the sheet value nor the alternative can be confirmed. Both are reported as DISCREPANT rather than VERIFIED. In both, the birth YEAR is agreed by both sources, so if only the year enters the system of record, both years are safe.
- Row 63 (Daniel Batson) also had a two-way conflict; it was resolved in the sheet's favour by a third source (Blueseum, Carlton FC's club history wiki), giving 2-of-3 for 08 May 1986.
- footywire.com and australianfootball.com returned persistent HTTP 503/403 through the proxy and could not be used. No row's outcome depends on them; no row is classified UNVERIFIABLE-FETCH.
- Excel float artifacts in `Birth year` ('1986.0') were parsed as int per instruction and are not counted as errors. In all 114 rows the year is internally consistent with the sheet's own date.
